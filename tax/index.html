Scope & sources

Uses 2026 ordinary-income brackets, preferential rates for net long-term gains and qualified dividends, standard deductions, age-65 additions, enhanced senior deduction phaseouts, Social Security taxation, and a simplified 3.8% net investment income tax. Assumes valid Social Security numbers for the senior deduction, no foreign-income adjustments, and that you are not another taxpayer’s dependent.

Excluded: state/local taxes, itemized deductions, tax credits, AMT, payroll and self-employment taxes, ACA subsidies, tax penalties, QBI, blind additions, special capital-gain rates, capital losses/carryovers, and deductions for tips, overtime, charitable gifts or car-loan interest. These can materially change the result. Income fields must be separate; do not enter the same income twice.

Withdrawals and conversions are assumed fully taxable. After-tax IRA basis requires Form 8606 pro-rata treatment, which is not modeled. RMDs cannot be converted; include their taxable amount in withdrawals first. Conversion tax is assumed paid from outside the converted balance. IRA withdrawals before 59½ may have penalties, and Roth five-year rules are not modeled.

2026 income normally informs 2028 IRMAA. Actual 2028 thresholds, filing status, enrollment and SSA life-changing-event relief may differ. Deductions below AGI—including the senior deduction—do not lower Medicare MAGI. The comparison shows surcharge differences, not base premiums or Part D plan premiums. Financial inputs stay on this browser unless you explicitly save a backup to your selected Google Drive account.
